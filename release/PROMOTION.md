# Launch copy and recording script

Not published. Record the application itself; do not substitute simulated UI or
claim unperformed performance benchmarks.

## Website copy

Guipper — creá y tocá visuales en tiempo real con shaders, audio y MIDI.

Software gratuito para visualistas. Combiná visuales generativas, imágenes y
videos en un canvas de nodos. Ajustá parámetros mientras tocás y construí tu
propia biblioteca de efectos.

Primary action: download the verified package for your platform.
Secondary actions: first visual in five minutes; report a problem; contribute.
Show the exact package version, validation matrix, release notes and SHA256.
Keep the contribution link hidden until the owner supplies the real destination.
Do not show a working download button while packages are still unvalidated drafts.

## Main video: 75 seconds

- 0–10: full-screen rings visual; title “Guipper”.
- 10–25: select Rings, change density and tint; show the result immediately.
- 25–40: choose audio input, show its meter and the matching visual reaction.
- 40–55: connect a MIDI knob and sweep one parameter.
- 55–65: mix an image/video with the generative output; crossfade between cues.
- 65–75: save the composition; show website URL and “Gratis / Free”.

## Three short recordings

Audio: 20 seconds, input meter beside the reacting visual.
MIDI: 20 seconds, physical knob and matching parameter visible together.
Live mix: 20 seconds, two sources, connection and resulting output.

Use your own media/audio or media cleared for publication. Keep original capture
files for edits. A physical-controller video requires a camera and MIDI hardware;
the automated regression harness cannot stand in for that recording.
